    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops when the other 54 are yours; the gold A Long Road needs 100% completion, so the roadmap ends there. Early estimates put the whole run at about 200 hours (PowerPyx) to 300 hours or more (Push Square).

    Missable trophies

    Built from the official descriptions and the reports available at launch. Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable — and the game has no mission replay, so the safety net is a manual save at the start of every chapter.

    Save advice

    Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies, and saving is blocked once one is used (PowerPyx, GameSpot and PC Gamer, via PlanetGTA6) — so play the whole run clean.

    About & sources

    This guide is a static web app: it loads only its own files, makes no network request, and stores your progress in your browser (localStorage). Nothing leaves your machine.

    Where the facts come from

      How to read this guide

      • Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list, as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it on 18 November 2026 — the two agreed on every name, description and grade.
      • Each trophy's guide is written in our own words for this guide. Where a specific place or step is not yet pinned down in a verified source, the guide says so instead of inventing one — a cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed.
      • Counts (250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, 18 meditation spots and the rest) are the progress targets PlayStation tracks, as PSNProfiles lists them.
      • Hidden trophies stay hidden on PlayStation until earned: 28 of the 55. Turn on “Hide story spoilers” and this guide hides their names and descriptions until you tick them earned.
      • No trophy needs online play, and there are no online trophies on this list.
